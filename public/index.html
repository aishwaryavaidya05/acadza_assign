How this works

Order: urgency = harm per student (a wrong answer key marks correct students wrong) × AI confidence × number of different students reporting. Cosmetic fixes rank lowest.

Safety: the AI only proposes. Nothing is written until you approve. Risky changes (answer keys, anything needing faculty) need a second click to confirm.

Built for long sessions: progress bar, search, keyboard-only flow, one-click Undo, and a bulk action that covers only low-risk text fixes. Answer-key changes always need individual review and a second confirming click.

Shortcuts: J/K next/previous   A approve   R reject   E edit   U undo

Decisions and the audit log are saved in this browser (localStorage). Results shown were precomputed by the engine unless a card says "Live".
